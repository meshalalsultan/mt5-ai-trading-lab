# Product delivery workflow
**Learn → Test → Build → Validate → Document → Demonstrate → Publish → Monetize**

Effective 2026-09-24: apply this cycle to practical trader usefulness and commercial/career artifacts. Platform debugging is not the project objective.

[Arabic operating guide](docs/ar/WORKFLOW.md) · [Goals](docs/ar/GOALS.md) · [Product plan](products/prompt-pack/PRODUCT-PLAN.ar.md)

One active product; five workdays and 25 production hours for the first delivery cycle. Build content, check factual correctness and usability, demonstrate the result, prepare distribution and an offer. Participant delays remain declared delays, not an excuse for more engineering.

Every task needs an audience, problem, deliverable, acceptance criterion and time limit. Review content once, conduct one limited user round and make one improvement. Remove a problematic feature or stop release rather than accepting a material error or entering open-ended technical investigation.

Record actual outcomes separately from plans, synthetic exercises, operator reports and purchases. Preserve old evidence and privacy. No outreach, live trading, profit promises, assumed sales or implied customer endorsements. Commercial messages require explicit authorization to send.

A day is complete when a usable artifact advances the product, portfolio or customer path, with a short status and one next action. Historical experiment protocols are references, not mandatory new tasks.
